Privacy Policy
Effective Date: August 5, 2026  ·  Last Updated: August 5, 2026
Short version: Digital Naap stores the customer records, measurements, and order data you enter locally on your device, and also syncs it to your secure account in the cloud (Firebase/Google Cloud) so it's backed up and available if you sign in on another device. This applies to both the Free and Pro tiers. We never sell your data.

1. Who We Are

Digital Naap is developed and published by Muhammad Yahya Aslam LLC, a registered limited liability company in New Mexico, USA.

2. Data We Collect

Digital Naap is a business tool for tailor shops. To do its job, it collects the following categories of data:

CategoryWhat's included
Your account (shop owner)Email address and/or phone number, used to sign in via Firebase Authentication
Customer records you enterName, phone number, email, address, and notes for each of your customers
Body measurementsMeasurement data you record per customer and outfit type
PhotosCustomer photos and your shop's logo, if you choose to add them
Financial / order dataOrder amounts, advance and balance payments, invoice line items, and your Pro subscription status
Crash diagnosticsAutomatically collected technical crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics) if the app encounters an error, to help us fix bugs
Advertising identifierCollected only on the Free tier, for ads served by Google AdMob (see Section 5)

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Digital Naap stores the data above in two places:

  • Locally, in a database on your device, for fast access even without an internet connection.
  • In the cloud, synced to a private, per-account space in Firebase/Google Cloud Firestore under your account only. This keeps your records backed up and lets you access them again if you reinstall the app or sign in on a different device.

This cloud sync applies to both the Free and Pro tiers — it is not a Pro-only feature. Your data is scoped to your account and is not accessible by other users of the app.

⚠️ We still recommend not entering more customer information than your business genuinely needs, and obtaining appropriate consent from your customers before storing their details (see Section 6).

4. Permissions We Request

Digital Naap requests the following on Android:

  • Internet / network state — required for account sign-in, cloud sync, and AdMob advertising (free tier).
  • Photo/media selection — used only when you choose to attach a customer photo or shop logo, via the device's standard photo picker. We do not access your photo library beyond the specific image you select.

Calling a customer or opening WhatsApp from the app hands off to your phone's dialer or WhatsApp app directly — Digital Naap does not need or request special permissions to do this.

5. Advertising (Free Tier)

The free version of Digital Naap displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, which uses an advertising identifier to serve ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

The Pro version of Digital Naap is completely ad-free, and does not use the advertising identifier.

6. Customer Consent

If you use Digital Naap to store your customers' personal information (name, phone number, measurements, etc.), you are responsible for obtaining any consent required by law in your jurisdiction before entering their data into the app.

7. In-App Purchases (Pro Subscription)

Digital Naap Pro is offered as a monthly subscription processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not store or have access to your payment card information — that is handled by Google Play.

8. WhatsApp Integration

The WhatsApp notification feature opens the WhatsApp application on your device with a pre-filled message template. We do not send messages on your behalf, store message logs, or have access to your WhatsApp account.

9. Crash Diagnostics

Digital Naap uses Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports (such as device model, OS version, and the technical error that occurred) if the app crashes or encounters an error. This helps us find and fix bugs. This data is technical/diagnostic in nature and is not linked to your customer records.

10. Data Deletion

You can delete individual customer records from within the app at any time. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us using the details below.

11. Children's Privacy

Digital Naap is a business tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
