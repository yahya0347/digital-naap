Privacy Policy
Effective Date: June 12, 2026  ·  Last Updated: June 12, 2026
Short version: Digital Naap stores all your data locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. Your customer records and measurements never leave your phone unless you explicitly back them up.

1. Who We Are

Digital Naap is developed and published by Muhammad Yahya Aslam LLC, a registered limited liability company in New Mexico, USA. Contact: ya987492@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

Digital Naap is designed as an offline-first application. All data you enter — including customer names, phone numbers, measurements, and payment records — is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device.

We do not collect:

  • Personal identifiable information (PII) from users
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash telemetry (unless explicitly disclosed below)

3. Customer Data You Enter

Any customer records, measurements, charges, and order history you create inside the app are:

  • Stored only on your local device
  • Never transmitted to our servers
  • Under your full ownership and control
  • Deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data
⚠️ We strongly recommend backing up your device regularly to prevent data loss, as we cannot recover locally-stored data on your behalf.

4. Permissions We Request

Digital Naap may request the following device permissions:

  • Phone (CALL_PHONE) — To enable direct dialing from customer cards. We do not log or store call records.
  • Internet — Required for optional cloud sync and AdMob advertising (free tier). No customer data is transmitted via internet access.
  • Storage / Media — To save or restore backups if the backup feature is used.

5. Advertising (Free Tier)

The free version of Digital Naap displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies to serve personalized ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

The Pro version of Digital Naap is completely ad-free.

6. In-App Purchases (Pro Subscription)

Digital Naap Pro is offered as a monthly subscription processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not store or have access to your payment card information. Subscription management is handled by Google Play. You may cancel anytime through your Google Play account settings.

7. WhatsApp Integration

The WhatsApp notification feature opens the WhatsApp application on your device with a pre-filled message template. We do not send messages on your behalf, store message logs, or have access to your WhatsApp account.

8. Children's Privacy

Digital Naap is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
